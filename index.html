About us

Alta Vola is a five-person acrobatics troupe from Seattle. We build human towers, throw and catch each other mid-air, and turn partner acrobatics into moments your guests will be talking about long after the event. From circus stages to cocktail hours, we tailor every performance to the space and the crowd.
